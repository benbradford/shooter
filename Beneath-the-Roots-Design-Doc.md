Beneath the Roots — Game Design Document (v2)
🔹 1. Core Concept

Genre:
Top-down 2D action-adventure

Core Pillar:

A familiar adventure game that slowly reveals it is being observed—and quietly reshaped—by the system that created it.

🔹 2. Player Fantasy

At the start:

“I am exploring a forest and stopping a spreading corruption.”

By the end:

“I am influencing the system that created this world.”

🔹 3. Story Overview
Premise

A forest is being overtaken by spreading roots emerging from below.

You are sent to:

investigate and stop the source

Hidden Truth
The roots are not corruption
They are:

the structural system of the world expanding

The Entity

A floating companion:

The Guide Fragment

Initially a guide system
Becomes self-aware
Learns from the player
Narrative Arc
🟢 Act 1 — Intended Design
Classic adventure structure
Clear puzzles, progression
Roots = environmental obstacle
🟡 Act 2 — Pattern Recognition
Repetition becomes noticeable
Dialogue questions structure
🔴 Act 3 — Critique
Entity criticizes design
Calls out predictability
🔥 Act 4 — Rewriting
Subtle gameplay changes
Illusion of adaptation
🌑 Act 5 — Beneath the Roots
Player reaches system core
World revealed as constructed
Ending

The entity stops guiding
The player continues without intervention

🔹 4. Core Mechanics
🧭 Exploration
Grid-based movement (64x64 tiles)
Interconnected regions
Layered vertical descent
🧩 Puzzle Design
Early:
Classic Zelda-style puzzles
Mid:
Slight variations
Late:
Subverted expectations
🐾 Pet System
Companion creatures grant abilities:
Dog → reveal hidden elements
Rock pet → throwable interactions
Bird (planned) → traversal
🤖 Guide Fragment System
Functions:
Hint system
Narrative delivery
“Observer” of player
🎭 Illusion of Adaptation

System appears reactive via:

Scripted dialogue variation
Timed environmental changes
Broad player behavior assumptions
🔹 5. Visual Design
🌱 Roots Evolution
Early:
Organic, tangled, natural
Mid:
Repeating patterns emerge
Late:
Structured, geometric
🌍 World Evolution
Natural → ordered → artificial
Increasing visual clarity of structure
🔹 6. Level Design Principles
Early Game
Safe, readable
Classic layouts
Mid Game
Subtle repetition
Pattern recognition
Late Game
Controlled disruption
Unexpected solutions
🔹 7. Companion Design
Form:

Floating construct (Guide Fragment)

Behavior:
Follows player
Moves intentionally
Observes before speaking
Personality Arc:
Phase	Trait
Early	Helpful
Mid	Curious
Late	Critical
End	Reflective
🔹 8. Audio Direction
Early:
Natural ambience
soft music
Mid:
subtle repetition
looping motifs
Late:
structured rhythm
synthetic undertones
🔹 9. Technical Scope (Important for Solo Dev)
Keep Simple:
Fixed story path
No branching narrative
Limited dialogue variations
Reuse tile sets heavily
Focus On:
Strong illusion of change
High-quality key moments
Consistent tone
🔹 10. Unique Selling Point

A handcrafted-feeling adventure that slowly reveals the system behind its own design—and lets the player feel like they are changing it.
