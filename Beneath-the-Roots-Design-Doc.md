# Beneath the Roots — Game Design Document

---

## 🎮 Core Concept

A 2D top-down action adventure game that begins as a light, Zelda-like experience and gradually transforms into a darker, unsettling, and self-aware narrative. Players start weak and curious, learning the world through movement and exploration, before gaining abilities that reveal deeper layers of both gameplay and reality.

---

# 🌿 Story

## Premise

You play as a young girl living in a quiet village at the edge of the Misty Moors. The world appears calm and ordinary, but subtle inconsistencies begin to emerge as you explore.

## Early Narrative

* You leave your home and are guided toward the Misty Moors.
* Wild cats behave erratically, forcing you to avoid rather than fight.
* At a shrine deep within the moors, you gain your first ability: the **Super Punch**.

## Mid Narrative

* You explore dungeons and rescue a missing dog, who becomes your first pet.
* NPCs begin to behave slightly oddly (pauses, repeated phrases, subtle awareness).
* The world starts to feel constructed rather than natural.

## Late Narrative

* Mechanics begin to break down (triggers misfire, geometry behaves inconsistently).
* Pets reveal hidden truths about the world.
* The player begins to question whether the world is real, simulated, or controlled.

## Themes

* Control vs free will
* Perception vs reality
* Systems breaking down
* Player as participant vs observer

---

# 📈 Progression

## Phase 1 — Learning (0–45 min)

* No combat initially
* Movement-focused gameplay (dodging wild cats)
* Introduction to environment navigation
* Gain **Super Punch** ability

## Phase 2 — Exploration (45–120 min)

* Introduction of first dungeon
* Rescue the dog → first pet
* Basic combat introduced
* Simple puzzles using environment and triggers

## Phase 3 — Expansion (2–3 hours)

* Additional pets unlocked
* More complex puzzles combining abilities
* Environmental storytelling increases
* Subtle glitches and inconsistencies begin

## Phase 4 — Breakdown (3–4 hours)

* Mechanics begin to fail or behave unpredictably
* Level design becomes unstable
* Narrative becomes more abstract and unsettling
* Player understanding is challenged

# ⚙️ Mechanics

## Movement

* 4-directional (or 8-directional) movement
* Core skill early on (no combat fallback)

## Combat

* Initially unavailable
* Introduced via **Super Punch**
* Close-range, impactful attack

## Environment Systems

* Walls (static / destructible)
* Gaps (require navigation or abilities)
* Stairs (verticality up to 3 levels)
* Water (blocks movement until swim ability unlocked)

## Triggers

* Step-based triggers (player enters tile)
* Enemy-based triggers (kill to activate)

## Interactive Elements

* Destructible objects (block paths)
* Switches (visible or hidden)
* Geometry changes (bridges, walls, water levels)

# 🐾 Pets (Ability System)

Pets are companions that grant unique abilities and unlock new gameplay possibilities.

## 🐕 Dog

* Ability: Bark
* Function:

  * Reveals hidden platforms and switches
  * Can scare certain enemies
* Acquisition:

  * Found in first dungeon
  * Escort quest → NPC captured → player keeps dog

---

## 🪨 Rock (Living Pet)

* Ability: Throw
* Function:

  * Can be thrown across gaps
  * Activates distant switches
  * Destroys enemies or objects out of reach

---

## 🐦 Bird (Planned)

* Ability: Carry / Glide
* Function:

  * Allows crossing large gaps
  * Introduces vertical traversal

# 🎭 Tone Shifts

## Phase 1 — Safe & Familiar

* Bright visuals
* Friendly NPCs
* Clear objectives
* Classic adventure feel

## Phase 2 — Subtle Unease

* Slight NPC oddities
* Repeated dialogue patterns
* Small environmental inconsistencies

## Phase 3 — Distortion

* Glitches in mechanics (timing, triggers)
* Visual anomalies
* Unexpected behaviour from systems

## Phase 4 — Breakdown

* Rules no longer reliable
* World feels unstable
* Narrative becomes ambiguous and unsettling

---

# 🎯 Design Principles

* Teach through gameplay, not text
* Start simple, layer complexity
* Reuse mechanics in new combinations
* Gradually subvert player expectations
* Keep early game grounded and accessible

---

# ⏱️ Target Experience

* Total playtime: ~4 hours
* Strong pacing curve:

  * Learn → Master → Question → Disrupt
